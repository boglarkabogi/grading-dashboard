Beadandó

\[
\left.
\begin{aligned}
17x &\equiv 17 \mod 375\\
19x &\equiv 9 \mod 358
\end{aligned}
\right\}
\]

\[
17x \equiv 17 \mod 375:
\]

17 prím és nem osztja 375-t, ezért

\[
x \equiv 1 \mod 375
\]

\[
19x \equiv 9 \mod 358:
\]

\[
358 = 18\cdot 19 + 16
\]

\[
19 = 1\cdot 16 + 3
\]

\[
16 = 5\cdot 3 + 1
\]

\[
1 = 16 - 5\cdot 3 = 16 - 5(19 - 1\cdot 16) =
\]

\[
= 6\cdot 16 - 5\cdot 19 = 6\cdot(358 - 18\cdot 19) -
\]

\[
-5\cdot 19 = 6\cdot 358 - 113\cdot 19
\]

\[
19^{-1} \equiv -113 \equiv 245 \mod 358
\]

\[
x \equiv 245\cdot 9 = 2205 \equiv 57 \mod 358
\]

Össz.:

\[
x \equiv 1 \mod 375
\]

\[
x \equiv 57 \mod 358
\]

\[
1 \to \text{ legyen } x = 1 + 375t
\]

\[
1 + 375t \equiv 57 \mod 358 \Rightarrow 375t \equiv 56 \mod 358
\]

\[
(375 \equiv 17 \mod 358) \to 17t \equiv 56 \mod 358
\]

\[
1 = 358 - 21\cdot 17
\]

\[
17^{-1} \equiv -21 \equiv 337 \mod 358
\]

\[
t \equiv 337\cdot 56 = 18872 \equiv 256 \mod 358
\]

\[
x = 1 + 375\cdot 256 = 96001
