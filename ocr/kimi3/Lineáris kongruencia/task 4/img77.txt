$358=18\cdot19+16$

$19=1\cdot16+3$

$16=5\cdot3+1$

$1=16-5\cdot3$

$1=16-5\cdot(19-16)$

$1=6\cdot16-5\cdot19$

$1=6(358-18\cdot19)-5\cdot19=6\cdot358-\underline{113}\cdot19$

$-113\cdot19 \equiv 1 \mod 358$
