4. Beadandó

a) $19x \equiv 14 \mod 297 \Rightarrow 19x + 297y = 14$

| $i$ | $r_i$ | $q_i$ | $x_i$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $-1$ | $19$ | — | $1$ |
| $0$ | $297$ | — | $0$ |
| $1$ | $19$ | $0$ | $1$ |
| $2$ | $12$ | $15$ | $-15$ |
| $3$ | $7$ | $1$ | $16$ |
| $4$ | $5$ | $1$ | $-31$ |
| $5$ | $2$ | $1$ | $47$ |
| $6$ | $1$ | $2$ | $-125$ |

$19 = 0 \cdot 297 + 19$  
$297 = 15 \cdot 19 + 12$  
$19 = 1 \cdot 12 + 7$  
$12 = 1 \cdot 7 + 5$  
$7 = 1 \cdot 5 + 2$  
$5 = 2 \cdot 2 + 1$  
$2 = 2 \cdot 1 + 0$

$q_i$ $r_i$

$19 \cdot -125 + 297 \cdot \square = 1 \quad / \cdot 14$  
$19 \cdot (-125 \cdot 14) + 297 \cdot (\square \cdot 14) = 14$

megoldás: $x = -125 \cdot 14 + 297k$

$-125 \cdot 14 = 1750$

$5 \cdot 297 = 1485$

$-1750 + 1485 = -265$  
$-265 + 297 = 32 \Rightarrow x \equiv 32 \mod 297$

b) $18x \equiv 17 \mod 482 \Rightarrow 18x + 482y = 17$

$482 = 26 \cdot 18 + 14$  
$18 = 1 \cdot 14 + 4$  
$14 = 3 \cdot 4 + 2$  
$4 = 2 \cdot 2 + 0$

mivel $2 \nmid 17$, ezért a kongruenciának nincs megoldása.
