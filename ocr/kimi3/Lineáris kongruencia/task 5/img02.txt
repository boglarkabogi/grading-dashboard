\[
13x+409y=13
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
13&=409\cdot 0+13\\
409&=31\cdot 13+6\\
13&=2\cdot 6+1\\
6&=6\cdot 1
\end{aligned}
\]

b) \(12x\equiv 7 \mod 302\)

\[
12x+302y=7
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
12&=0\cdot 302+12\\
302&=25\cdot 12+2\\
12&=2\cdot 6+0
\end{aligned}
\]

| \(i\) | \(r_i\) | \(q_i\) | \(x_i\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(-1\) | \(12\) | – | \(1\) |
| \(0\) | \(302\) | – | \(0\) |
| \(1\) | \(12\) | \(0\) | \(1\) |
| \(2\) | \(2\) | \(25\) | \(-25\) |

\[
12\cdot(-25)\equiv 2 \mod 302
\]

\[
12\cdot(-25)+302y=7
\]

\[
\Rightarrow 12\cdot(-25)+302y=2 \Rightarrow \text{nincs egész megoldása}
