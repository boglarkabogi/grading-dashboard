a/  
\[
11x \equiv 2 \mod 238
\]

| \(i\) | \(v_i\) | \(q_i\) | \(x_i\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(-1\) | \(11\) | – | \(1\) |
| \(0\) | \(238\) | – | \(0\) |
| \(1\) | \(7\) | \(21\) | \(1\) |
| \(2\) | \(4\) | \(1\) | \(-1\) |
| \(3\) | \(3\) | \(1\) | \(2\) |
| \(4\) | \(1\) | \(3\) | \(-7\) |

\[
11=0\cdot 238+11
\]
\[
238=21\cdot 11+7
\]
\[
11=1\cdot 7+4
\]
\[
7=1\cdot 4+3
\]
\[
4=1\cdot 3+\boxed{1}
\]
\[
3=3\cdot 1+0
\]

\[
1=4-1\cdot 3=4-(7-1\cdot 4)=2\cdot 4-7
\]
\[
=2(11-1\cdot 7)-7=2\cdot 11-3\cdot 7
\]
\[
=2\cdot 11-3(238-21\cdot 11)=\underline{65}\cdot 11-3\cdot 238
\]

\[
x\equiv 2\cdot 65=\underline{130}\mod 238
\]
