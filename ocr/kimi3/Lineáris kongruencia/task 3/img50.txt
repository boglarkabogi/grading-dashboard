**Bal oldal:**

$11x \equiv 2 \mod 238$

$$238 = 21 \cdot 11 + 7$$

$$11 = 1 \cdot 7 + 4$$

$$7 = 1 \cdot 4 + 3$$

$$4 = 1 \cdot 3 + 1$$

$$3 = 3 \cdot \boxed{1} + 0$$

$$1 = 4 - 1 \cdot 3$$

$$1 = 4 - 1 \cdot (7 - 1 \cdot 4)$$

$$1 = 2 \cdot 4 - 7$$

$$1 = 2 \cdot (11 - 1 \cdot 7) - 7$$

$$1 = 2 \cdot 11 - 3 \cdot 7$$

$$1 = 2 \cdot 11 - 3(238 - 21 \cdot 11)$$

$$1 = 65 \cdot 11 - 3 \cdot 238$$

$$65 \cdot 11 \equiv 1 \mod 238$$

$$65 \cdot \frac{2}{1} = \underline{130}$$

**Jobb oldal:**

$16x \equiv 15 \mod 490$

$$490 = 30 \cdot 16 + 10$$

$$16 = 1 \cdot 10 + 6$$

$$10 = 1 \cdot 6 + 4$$

$$6 = 1 \cdot 4 + 2$$

$$4 = 2 \cdot \boxed{2} + 0$$

$(16, 490) \nmid 15 \Rightarrow$ nincs megoldás, $x \in \mathbb{Z}_+$
