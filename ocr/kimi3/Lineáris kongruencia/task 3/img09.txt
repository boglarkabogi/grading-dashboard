# Házi feladat

Oldja meg az alábbi lineáris kongruenciákat:

$$11x \equiv 2 \mod 238 \quad \text{és} \quad 16x \equiv 15 \mod 490$$

---

**$11x \equiv 2 \mod 238$**

$$238 = 21 \cdot 11 + 7$$
$$11 = 1 \cdot 7 + 4$$
$$7 = 1 \cdot 4 + 3$$
$$4 = 1 \cdot 3 + 1 \quad \Rightarrow \text{lnko}(11, 238) = 1$$
$$3 = 3 \cdot 1 + 0 \quad \Rightarrow \text{egy megoldása van}$$

| $i$ | $r_i$ | $q_i$ | $x_i$ | $y_i$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $-1$ | 238 | — | 0 | 1 |
| 0 | 11 | — | 1 | 0 |
| 1 | 7 | 21 | $0-1\cdot 21=$ $-21$ | $1-0\cdot 21=$ $1$ |
| 2 | 4 | 1 | $1-(-21)\cdot 1=$ $22$ | $0-1\cdot 1=$ $-1$ |
| 3 | 3 | 1 | $-21-22\cdot 1=$ $-43$ | $1-(-1)\cdot 1=$ $2$ |
| 4 | 1 | 1 | $22-(-43)\cdot 1=$ $65$ | $-1-2\cdot 1=$ $-3$ |
|  | 0 | 3 |  |  |

$$11 \cdot 65 + 238 \cdot (-3) = 1$$
$$11 \cdot \boxed{130} + 238 \cdot (-6) = 1$$

---

**$16x \equiv 15 \mod 490$**

$$490 = 30 \cdot 16 + 10$$
$$16 = 1 \cdot 10 + 6$$
$$10 = 1 \cdot 6 + 4$$
$$6 = 1 \cdot 4 + \boxed{2} \Rightarrow \text{lnko}(16, 490) = 2$$
$$4 = 2 \cdot 2 + 0 \quad \Rightarrow \text{egyért nincs megoldása}$$
