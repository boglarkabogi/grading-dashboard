①  
\[
19x\equiv 16 \mod 470
\]
\[
19x+470g=16
\]

| \(i\) | \(a_i\) | \(q_i\) | \(x_i\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| \(-1\) | \(19\) | — | \(1\) |
| \(0\) | \(470\) | — | \(0\) |
| \(1\) | \(19\) | \(0\) | \(1\) |
| \(2\) | \(14\) | \(24\) | \(-24\) |
| \(3\) | \(5\) | \(1\) | \(25\) |
| \(4\) | \(4\) | \(2\) | \(-74\) |
| \(5\) | \(1\) | \(1\) | \(99\) |

\[
\begin{aligned}
19&=0\cdot470+19\\
470&=24\cdot19+14\\
19&=1\cdot14+5\\
14&=2\cdot5+4\\
5&=1\cdot4+1\\
4&=4\cdot1+0\\
1&=0
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
19\cdot99+470g=1 \ \ /\cdot16
\]
\[
16\cdot(19\cdot99)+16\cdot470g=16
\]
\[
x=16\cdot99+470k
\]

16x\equiv 5 \mod 202

16x+202g=5
