**1.)** $13x \equiv 19 \mod 414$

$\text{LNKO}(414, 13) = 1$

$13x + 414y = 19$

$x_i = x_{i-2} - (q_{i-1} \cdot x_{i-1})$
$y_i = y_{i-2} - (q_{i-1} \cdot y_{i-1})$

$414 = 31 \cdot 13 + 11$
$13 = 1 \cdot 11 + 2$
$11 = 5 \cdot 2 + 1$
$2 = 2 \cdot 1 + 0$
