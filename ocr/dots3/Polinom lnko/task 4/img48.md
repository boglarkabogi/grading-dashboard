$$f = x^4+4x^3+x^2+2 \in \mathbb{Z}_5[x]$$
$$g = x^5+x^4+3x^3+x^2+x+4 \in \mathbb{Z}_5[x]$$

$$x^5+x^4+3x^3+x^2+x+4 = (x^4+4x^3+x^2+2) \cdot (x+2) + x^3-x^2-x$$

$$\frac{f}{g} = \frac{x^5+x^4+3x^3+x^2+x+4}{x^4+4x^3+x^2+2} = x+2$$
$$\downarrow$$
$$g \cdot x = x^5+4x^4+x^3+2x$$
$$h = f - g \cdot x = -3x^4+2x^3+x^2-x+4 = 2x^4+2x^3+x^2-x+4$$
$$g \cdot 2 = 2x^4+3x^3+2x^2+4$$
$$h - g \cdot 2 = -x^3-x^2-x$$

$$x^4+4x^3+x^2+2 = -(x^3+x^2+x) \cdot (-x-3) + \boxed{-3x^2-3x+2}$$
$$\frac{f}{g} = \frac{x^4+4x^3+x^2+2}{-x^3-x^2-x} = -x-3$$
$$g \cdot (-x) = x^4+x^3+x^2$$
$$h = f - g \cdot (-x) = 3x^3+2$$
$$g \cdot (-3) = 3x^3+3x^2+3x$$
$$h - g \cdot (-3) = -3x^2-3x+2$$

$$-x^3-x^2-x = -3x^2-3x+2 \cdot 2x + 0$$
$$\frac{f}{g} = \frac{-x^3-x^2-x}{-3x^2-3x+2} = \frac{4x^3+4x^2+4x}{2x^2+2x+2}$$
$$g \cdot 2x = 4x^3+4x^2+4x$$
$$f - g \cdot 2x = 0$$

$$\text{Megoldás: } -3x^2-3x+2 = 2x^2+2x+2$$
