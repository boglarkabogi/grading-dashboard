\(u \cdot (x^2 + x + 1) + v \cdot (x^2 - 1) = x - 1 \quad u, v \in \mathbb{Q}[x]\)

\(f(x) = 1 \cdot g(x) + r_1(x) \implies (x^2 + x + 1) = 1 \cdot (x^2 - 1) + (x + 2) \quad r_1(x) = x + 2\)

\(g(x) = (x - 2) \cdot r_1(x) + r_2(x) \implies x^2 - 1 = (x^2 - x) + 3 \implies x^2 - 1 = (x - 2)(x + 2) + 3 \quad r_2(x) = 3\)

\(1 = \frac{1}{3} \left[ g(x) - (x - 2) \cdot r_1(x) \right] \implies 1 = \frac{1}{3} \left[ g(x) - (x - 2) \cdot (f(x) - g(x)) \right] \implies 1 = \frac{1}{3} \left[ g(x) - (x - 2) \cdot f(x) + (x - 2)g(x) \right] \iff\)

\(1 = \frac{1}{3} \left[ (x - 1)g(x) - (x - 2)f(x) \right] \iff -(x - 2) \cdot f(x) + (x - 1) \cdot g(x) = 1 \iff -(x - 2) \cdot f(x) \cdot (x - 1) + (x - 1)^2 \cdot g(x) = x - 1\)
