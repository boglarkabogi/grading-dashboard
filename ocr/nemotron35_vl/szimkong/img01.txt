x = 15k + 13

15k + 13 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}
k + 6 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}
k \equiv 0 \pmod{7}
k = 7m
x = 15(7m) + 13 = 105m + 13
x \equiv 13 \pmod{105}
